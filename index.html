Reading sign

Animals leave prints as they travel. Fresh sign is dark and fades over about a minute — the paired marks point the way the animal was heading, and a third mark between them means it was running. Wounded animals leave blood as well; follow it and they can still be recovered.

How to hunt

WASD or the arrows to move · hold Shift to run · E toggles auto-run (then Shift walks)
Stand still to disappear. An animal is far less likely to notice you when you are not moving, and your aim settles too. Running is the loudest and most visible thing you can do.
Look: move the mouse. Click once to lock the mouse for smooth 360° spinning. If your browser won't lock it, you can still turn all the way around with ←→ or by pushing the cursor against the left/right edge of the screen.
Left-click fires · Right-click or F raises the scope (a tap, not a hold) · Space holds your breath while scoped · R reloads · Middle-click, the wheel or Q swaps rifle and bow · K hides the on-screen controls · Esc pauses
Left stick walks — push it all the way to run. Stand still and animals are far less likely to notice you. Drag anywhere else to look. AIM raises the scope, FIRE shoots, SWAP GUN switches rifle and bow, and holding HOLD BREATH while scoped steadies your aim.

Rules of the hunt

Tags. Each hunt licenses you for certain species only. Shooting anything else costs you $200 and a score penalty — check the tag list before you pull. Self-defence is always legal: an animal charging you can be shot on any hunt, no penalty, and it still scores.

Senses. Animals see you when you're upright and moving in front of them, hear you when you run, and smell you when the wind blows from you to them — watch the wind arrow on the compass. A rifle shot spooks everything for 150 m; the bow is nearly silent.

Recovery. Wounded animals bleed and leave a trail you can follow. After a kill, walk up to the animal to tag it for bonus cash.

Danger. Bears charge if you wound them or get close. In a hunt, a bear that reaches you ends the hunt. Wounded boars charge too and hit for real damage.

Trophies. Trophy-class animals carry oversized antlers, pay double, and several hunts require them.
